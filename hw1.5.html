Batman (1989)

Main characters

Bruce Wayne (Batman) played by Michael Keaton.Bruce Wayne is a wealthy businessman and philanthropist in Gotham City. After witnessing the murder of his parents as a child, he dedicates his life to fighting crime as Batman, the city's mysterious masked vigilante.

The Joker. He was played by Jack Nicholson.Originally the gangster Jack Napier, the Joker becomes Gotham's most dangerous criminal after a chemical accident transforms his appearance. He thrives on chaos and becomes Batman's greatest enemy.

Vicki Vale was played by Kim Basinger. Vicki Vale is a talented and ambitious photojournalist who investigates Batman's activities. She develops a close relationship with Bruce Wayne and becomes involved in the conflict between Batman and the Joker.

Alexander Know. Played by Robert Wuhl. Alexander Knox is a Gotham newspaper reporter obsessed with uncovering the truth about Batman. His investigations help bring the vigilante into the public spotlight.

Commissioner James Gordon. Played by Pat Hingle. Commissioner Gordon is a senior Gotham City police official who works to combat crime and eventually allies with Batman against the Joker's reign of terror.

Harvey Dent was played by Billy Dee Williams. Harvey Dent serves as Gotham City's district attorney. He is committed to fighting organized crime and is one of the city's strongest advocates for justice.

Alfred Pennyworth was played by Michael Gough. Alfred Pennyworth is Bruce Wayne's loyal butler, confidant, and father figure. He supports Bruce's mission as Batman and helps maintain the secrecy of his dual identity.
